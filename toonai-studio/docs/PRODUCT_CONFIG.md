- ₹49 = 100
- ₹99 = 200

- FREE = ₹0 / 10 welcome credits
- BASIC = ₹99/month / 30 credits
- CREATOR = ₹199/month / 100 credits
- PRO = ₹399/month / 250 credits

Generation:
- 5 sec = 1
- 10 sec = 2
- 15 sec = 3
- 30 sec = 5
